💡 Financial Wisdom for Adults

  • 50/30/20 Rule: Aim for 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings/debt repayment.
  • Housing: Try to keep housing costs under 30% of your income.
  • Emergency Fund: Build 3-6 months of expenses for unexpected situations.
  • Retirement: Start early! Even small contributions compound over time.
  • Debt Strategy: Pay off high-interest debt first (avalanche method) or smallest balances first (snowball method).
