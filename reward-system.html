Welcome to Your InnerCents Pet!

🎉 Welcome! You've just adopted your first InnerCents Pet!

How it works:

  • 🪙 Earn InnerCents Coins by completing lessons and activities
  • 🍎 Use coins to feed, play with, and customize your pet
  • 📈 Keep your pet happy, fed, and energized to help it grow!
  • 🌟 Your pet will evolve through 3 stages: Baby → Teen → Adult
  • 🎓 Complete lessons to increase your Learning Progress and unlock rewards

Don't worry! Your pet won't die or run away. Stats decrease slowly, so you can take breaks!
