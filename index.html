The Problem: An Overwhelmed System

With states now mandating financial literacy, schools are scrambling for effective curriculum. Existing options are disconnected from students' emotional and cultural realities, leaving teachers with inadequate tools for a complex subject.

The Solution: A Holistic, Empathetic Approach

InnerCents is the only K-12 curriculum built on the understanding that financial decisions are emotional. We combine practical skills with social-emotional learning and economic justice.

Social-Emotional Learning

Address money-related stress and build emotional resilience.

Contextual Financial Literacy

Teach practical skills grounded in diverse cultural and community contexts.

Economic Justice

Analyze systemic inequities and empower students to advocate for change.

About the Founder

Cristal A. Ellis

InnerCents was founded by Cristal A. Ellis, a Behavioral Health Specialist from the U.S. Army turned educator and strategist. Her unique background in human-centered support systems and education fuels our mission to deliver a curriculum that addresses not just the 'how' but the emotional 'why' of financial well-being.
