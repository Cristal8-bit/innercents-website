Signature Activity: Values & Visions

An interpersonal reflection exercise designed to help students connect their core values to their future aspirations and financial habits.

1. Value Card Sort

In small groups, students sort through cards listing core values (e.g., Community, Security, Adventure) to identify what matters most to them individually.

2. Storytelling Circle

Each student shares a short personal story or a family memory that illustrates why one of their chosen values is important to them.

3. Vision Boarding

Students create a simple "vision board" or a written statement describing a future life that honors their core values. What does that life look like?

4. Connecting Values to Actions

The group discusses: "What's one small financial action you could take today that aligns with your vision? What's an action that might conflict?"

5. Group Reflection

Facilitated dialogue on how understanding our personal "why" can make financial planning feel more meaningful and less like a chore.

6. Personal Commitment

The activity concludes with each student writing down one personal, value-driven financial goal, fostering a sense of ownership and purpose.
