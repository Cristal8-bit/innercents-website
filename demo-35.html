Lesson Focus: Indigenous Economics & Community Value

This lesson explores how different cultures have valued resources and exchanged goods outside of traditional money systems.

Activity: What is "Value"?

Consider the story of the Three Sisters—corn, beans, and squash—an agricultural practice of many Indigenous communities. The three crops support each other's growth. The corn provides a stalk for the beans to climb, the beans provide nitrogen to the soil, and the squash provides ground cover to prevent weeds.

Question: Besides food, what "value" do the Three Sisters provide to the community?

  • Value of teamwork and mutual support.
  • Value of sustainability and caring for the land.
  • Value of knowledge passed through generations.

SEL Focus (Beyond Money): This helps students recognize "value" in relationships, community, and well-being, not just in monetary terms.
